# Sign & Sky app: privacy policy (draft for the team to review and publish)

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Sign & Sky records farmers' weather signs from Wolaita, South Ethiopia, tests them against recorded
weather and returns the result to farmers. This policy covers the Sign & Sky mobile app.

## What the app keeps on your phone
- Your language choice and whether the voice is muted.
- A copy of the sign library, average rainfall figures and Amharic voice recordings, so the app works
  without internet.
- A sign you are writing (draft) and signs waiting to be sent, with any photo or voice note you added.

This stays on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

## What is sent to Sign & Sky, and when
Only when you tap **Send the sign**: the sign, its description, the date, the region and place you
typed, and optionally your name, village, crop, a photo and a voice note. It is sent to the Sign & Sky
website so researchers can review and test the sign. Your name and village are never published; only
signed-in researchers can see them. A sign may be published, without your name, after review.

When the phone is online the app also downloads the public sign library, rainfall figures and Amharic
voice files from the Sign & Sky website. These requests carry no information about you beyond what any
internet request carries (such as your network address).

## Permissions
- Microphone: only when you tap Speak or record a voice note.
- Speech recognition: when you tap Speak, your phone's own speech service turns your words into text.
  Depending on the phone, that service may send the audio to its provider (for example Google or Apple).
- Camera and photos: only when you add a photo to a sign.
- The app does not read your location, contacts or advertising identifier, and has no advertising or
  analytics.

## Your choices
You can use the app without sharing anything. To ask for a shared sign to be removed, contact the team
with the reference code shown after sending: [team contact address to be added].
