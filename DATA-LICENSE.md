# Licence for data, text and media

The source code in this repository is released under the MIT licence (`LICENSE`).

Everything else is released under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)**,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/, unless a file says otherwise:

- the indigenous weather knowledge records, translations and spoken advice (`src/data`, `src/dictionaries`, `supabase/seed`);
- the pre-registration, analysis results and figures (`research/`);
- the documentation.

**Credit the community.** The weather knowledge was shared by farmers and elders of W/Dekeya, Wareza and Yakima
kebeles, Offa Woreda, Wolaita, Ethiopia. When you reuse it, credit the community and cite this repository. Use it in
ways that benefit the community it came from, following the CARE principles for Indigenous data governance
(https://www.gida-global.org/care).

**Exceptions.**
- Fonts in `src/fonts` are under the SIL Open Font Licence (`src/fonts/OFL.txt`).
- Rainfall series in `research/results-2026-11-13/data` are derived from CHIRPS (Climate Hazards Center, UC Santa
  Barbara) and ERA5-Land (Copernicus Climate Change Service, ECMWF) and remain under those providers' terms; cite the
  original datasets.
- Photographs in `public/media` may be reused only with the team's permission.
